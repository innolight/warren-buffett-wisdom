# Institutional Imperative

## Definition

The tendency of corporate managements to follow what their peers do — even when it is irrational — and the corresponding difficulty of acting counter-institutionally. Not yet named in the 1977 letter, but introduced in nascent form via the discussion of insurance underwriting discipline.

## How it's discussed in sources

- [[berkshire-1977-letter]] — early articulation: "Unusual managerial discipline will be required, as it runs counter to normal institutional behavior to let the other fellow take away business - even at foolish prices." The discipline of accepting reduced volume in soft markets is the use case.

Later letters give this concept its formal name and broader application — page to be expanded as those sources are ingested.
