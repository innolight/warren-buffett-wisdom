# Look-Through Earnings

## Definition

The earnings *attributable* to a partial-equity stake — the holder's pro-rata share of the underlying business's earnings — as distinct from the dividend income that actually shows up in the holder's reported P&L. The 1977 letter doesn't yet use the term, but the concept is introduced explicitly.

## How it's discussed in sources

- [[berkshire-1977-letter]] — the Capital Cities example: $10.9M invested; ~$1.3M of attributable 1977 earnings; only $40,000 in cash dividend reflected in Berkshire's operating earnings. Buffett notes the gap as a feature, not a defect — outstanding partial holdings may have "negligible impact on our operating earnings" in the short term, while building substantial economic value.

Later letters formalize this idea and report look-through figures explicitly. Page to be expanded then.
