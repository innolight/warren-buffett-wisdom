# Warren E. Buffett

Chairman of [[berkshire-hathaway]] since the 1965 change in corporate control. Author of the annual shareholder letter that anchors this wiki.

## Views and contributions

The 1977 letter establishes the framework Buffett will reuse for decades:

- **Return on equity, not EPS, as the management scorecard.** EPS growth is artifactual when equity itself is growing — see [[return-on-equity-yardstick]].
- **The four-criteria acquisition test:** understandable business, favorable long-term prospects, honest and competent management, attractive price. Applied identically to marketable equities and whole-company purchases. See [[acquisition-criteria]].
- **Tailwinds matter more than effort.** "Being in businesses where tailwinds prevail rather than headwinds" — a lesson "learned, and unfortunately sometimes re-learned." See [[tailwinds-vs-headwinds]].
- **Counter-institutional discipline.** Willingness to accept reduced insurance volume in soft markets, even though "it runs counter to normal institutional behavior to let the other fellow take away business — even at foolish prices." Seed of the [[institutional-imperative]].
- **Look-through ownership economics.** Reported dividend income understates the value of partial-equity stakes; what matters is attributable earnings. See [[look-through-earnings]].
- **Better through non-control.** Capital Cities used as the case study for an "unorthodox view": "we can obtain a better management result through non-control than control."

## Notable quotes
