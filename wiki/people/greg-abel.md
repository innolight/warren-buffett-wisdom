# Greg Abel

Vice Chairman of Berkshire Hathaway (non-insurance operations), appointed in early 2018 alongside [[ajit-jain]] (insurance). Abel built his Berkshire career running [[midamerican-energy]] (now Berkshire Hathaway Energy), the regulated utility holding acquired in 2000. The Vice Chairman designation formalized the succession architecture publicly: Abel oversees the vast non-insurance operating businesses (railroads, utilities, manufacturing, retail, services), while Buffett and Munger focus on investments and capital allocation.

## Views and contributions

Abel's track record at BHE reflects the capital-allocation discipline that Berkshire prizes broadly: heavy reinvestment in capital-intensive regulated assets with predictable long-duration returns, no dividend extraction, and operational compounding over decades. Under his tenure, BHE achieved leading U.S. renewable energy scale (top wind installer, significant solar) while holding Iowa consumer rates essentially flat for years — a record consistent with the Berkshire social-compact framing for regulated infrastructure. See [[midamerican-energy]].
