# Ken Chace

Manager of Berkshire's textile operations. The 1977 letter credits his efforts after the 1965 change in corporate control with generating the capital from textiles that funded the acquisition and expansion of Berkshire's insurance operation — the financial bridge from the legacy business to the future engine.
