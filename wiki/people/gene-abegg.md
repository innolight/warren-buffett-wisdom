# Gene Abegg

Founder of [[illinois-national-bank]] (1931, with $250,000 of capital). Still Chairman and operating the bank in 1977 at age 80, described by Buffett as "a banking operation without peer." Asked for a successor late in 1977; Peter Jeffrey was named President/CEO effective March 1, 1978. Abegg remained Chairman.
