# George Young

Manager of [[national-indemnity]]'s reinsurance department. The 1977 letter credits him with improved underwriting performance during 1977 — combined ratio of 107.1 was still unsatisfactory, but trending downward through the year. Notes that reinsurance generates "unusually high funds for investment as a percentage of premium volume."
