# John Ringwalt

Architect of Berkshire's homestate insurance operation: five companies as of late 1977 (Cornhusker Casualty, Lakeland Fire and Casualty, Texas United, Insurance Company of Iowa, Kansas Fire and Casualty). Personally manages Cornhusker, which has recorded combined ratios below 100 in six of its seven full years.

Homestate net premium volume reached $23M in 1977, up from $5.5M three years earlier. All four full-year operations achieved combined ratios below 100.
