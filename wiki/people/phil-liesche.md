# Phil Liesche

Head of underwriting at [[national-indemnity]]. Credited in the 1977 letter (and again, implicitly, with "truly outstanding results") for an "extraordinary underwriting achievement" in National Indemnity's traditional auto and general liability lines during 1977 — large volume gains paired with excellent margins. Worked alongside Roland Miller (underwriting) and Bill Lyons (claims).
