# Capital Cities Communications

Media and broadcasting company. Berkshire invested $10.9M during 1977; yearend market value $13.2M (220,000 shares).

Buffett's framing in the 1977 letter: "Capital Cities possesses both extraordinary properties and extraordinary management … To purchase, directly, properties such as Capital Cities owns would cost in the area of twice our cost of purchase via the stock market." Earnings attributable to the purchased shares were ~$1.3M vs. only $40,000 in cash dividends — an early articulation of [[look-through-earnings]].

Eight years before the famous 1985 ABC acquisition deal.
