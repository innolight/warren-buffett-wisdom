# Illinois National Bank

Bank in Rockford, Illinois. Founded by [[gene-abegg]] in 1931 with $250,000 of capital. Acquired by [[berkshire-hathaway]] in 1969.

## History

First full year of operation in 1931: earnings of $8,782. No new capital contributed to the bank since founding. Since Berkshire's 1969 acquisition through 1977, the bank paid $20M in dividends to Berkshire. 1977 earnings of $3.6M — more than achieved by many banks two or three times its size, on an ROA roughly **3x most large banks**.

Buffett's framing: this record was achieved while the bank paid maximum allowed rates to savers and maintained "low risk and exceptional liquidity."

## Why it matters

A cash cow with exceptional underwriting discipline and capital efficiency. Demonstrates the same pattern as See's — high return on the original capital, with cash extractable to fund other allocation. (Berkshire was later forced to divest under the Bank Holding Company Act amendments; not yet relevant in 1977.)

## Leadership

[[gene-abegg]] founded the bank in 1931 and remained Chairman at age 80 as of the 1977 letter. Peter Jeffrey, formerly President/CEO of American National Bank of Omaha, was named President and CEO effective March 1, 1978.
