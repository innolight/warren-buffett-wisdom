# H.H. Brown Shoe Company

New England footwear manufacturer acquired by Berkshire in 1991. Frank Rooney, a long-time Buffett acquaintance, ran the business. First in a series of shoe acquisitions that eventually included Lowell Shoe (1992 add-on) and Dexter Shoe (1993 — now recognized as Buffett's worst acquisition). H.H. Brown itself performed well; Dexter, by contrast, became the cautionary case for the entire shoe group.
