# See's Candies

West Coast confectioner, 99%-owned subsidiary of [[blue-chip-stamps]]. Purchased by Blue Chip at the beginning of 1972. Run by [[chuck-huggins]].

## History

Acquired in 1972. Pre-tax operating earnings grew from $4.2M (1972) to $12.6M (1977) — a 3x in five years — with "little additional capital investment," in an industry experiencing "practically no unit growth."

## Why it matters

The archetypal high-return-on-tangible-capital business in the Berkshire universe: pricing power and brand strength producing earnings growth that vastly outpaces capital reinvested. The 1977 letter doesn't yet frame See's in these terms explicitly, but the figures are doing the work — earnings tripling without significant new capital, in a flat-volume industry, is the empirical fingerprint of the kind of business Berkshire will later valorize.
