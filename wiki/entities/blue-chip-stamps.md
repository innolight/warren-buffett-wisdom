# Blue Chip Stamps

Trading-stamp company turned holding entity, a Berkshire affiliate. Berkshire's equity interest reached approximately 36.5% by yearend 1977. Notable principally as the conduit through which Berkshire owned [[sees-candies]] (99%) and [[wesco-financial]] (80%).

## History

In 1977, Blue Chip earned approximately $12.9M from operations plus $4.1M of realized securities gains. Berkshire continued increasing its stake during the year.

## Why it matters

A structurally important Berkshire affiliate during this era: not consolidated into Berkshire's operating earnings except via the proportional interest, but the home of two outstanding subsidiaries. Capital gains realized by Blue Chip flow through to Berkshire's operating earnings figure (in 1977: $1.43 of Berkshire's $22.54 EPS came from this channel).
