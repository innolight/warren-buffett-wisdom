# Cypress Insurance Company

Worker's Compensation insurer based in South Pasadena, California. Acquired by Berkshire for cash in the final days of 1977. ~$12.5M in 1977 premium volume (not included in 1977 Berkshire results). Run by Milt Thornton, President since 1968.

The 1977 letter notes Cypress will operate independently from National Indemnity's own California Worker's Comp operation, using different marketing strategies.
