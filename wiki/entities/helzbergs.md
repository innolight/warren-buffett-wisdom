# Helzberg Diamonds

Kansas City jewelry retailer acquired by Berkshire in 1995. Sourced by Barnett Helzberg approaching Buffett directly. Jeff Comment became CEO and later restored margins after a mid-1990s stumble. Helzberg extended Berkshire's referral-chain acquisition pattern: Barnett Helzberg himself introduced the Ben Bridge Jeweler acquisition (2000) to Berkshire, mirroring the furniture chain (NFM → R.C. Willey → Star → Jordan's).
